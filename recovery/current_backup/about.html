Corporate Overview

About MoviSabio

Transforming fragmented territorial data into actionable spatial intelligence. We are dedicated to scaling AI, IoT, and quantum-ready digital twin ecosystems globally.

🎯

Our Global Mission

To empower municipalities, infrastructure operators, and regional governments across all continents to transition from reactive management to predictive, evidence-based spatial governance. We eliminate the barrier of expensive hardware lock-in by providing scalable, software-first intelligence platforms.

🌍

Our Long-Term Vision

To establish MoviSabio as the world's leading Territorial Intelligence Operating System (TIOS). A unified digital foundation that connects traffic corridors, public transit networks, environmental sensors, and energy grids into a cohesive, sustainable digital twin.

Values & Philosophy

Core Engineering Principles

The standards that guide our algorithmic development, data governance, and public sector partnerships.

🛡️

Deterministic Safety Envelopes

Artificial intelligence must never compromise public safety. Our reinforcement learning models operate strictly inside proven classical engineering bounds (MAXBAND), with physical fail-safe limits locked into controller hardware.

🔒

Privacy by Design

We believe smart cities should not become surveillance states. Computer vision telemetry is processed locally at the edge. No facial recognition, vehicle owner indexing, or private video recordings are stored centrally.

🌱

Measurable Climate Action

Every algorithmic decision is measured against carbon and air quality impact. By cutting unnecessary intersection wait times, our platform directly reduces vehicle idling and supports UN Sustainable Development Goals 11 and 13.

Roadmap Horizon

Strategic Corporate Objectives (2026–2030)

Our multi-year strategy bridges non-dilutive academic grants with enterprise SaaS deployments across Latin America and Europe:

1. Simulation Validation

Complete baseline benchmarking across Campinas and Montevideo corridors in SUMO, proving up to 45% vehicular delay reduction in software.

2. EU Grant Consortiums

Secure research partnerships with European universities under Horizon Europe and EIT Urban Mobility to validate cross-border digital twins.

3. Municipal Expansion

Execute pilot corridor Letters of Intent (LOIs) with municipal transit authorities across mid-sized cities in Brazil, Uruguay, and Southern Europe.
