Governance & Culture

Institutional Governance Charter

MoviSabio is built on scientific transparency, strict algorithmic auditability, and responsible public-private collaboration:

Algorithmic Auditability & XAI

Every AI actuation decision is logged with Explainable AI (XAI) feature importance metrics, allowing municipal traffic engineers to inspect why specific signal plans were selected and guarantee fail-safe fallback.

Data Sovereignty & Privacy

Municipal spatial telemetry remains the exclusive property of the contracting authority. We strictly adhere to GDPR (EU) and LGPD (Brazil) standards—never selling or commercializing citizen mobility records.

Open Scientific Benchmarking

We actively publish our SUMO simulation topologies and performance metrics to foster academic peer review and accelerate global research in resilient urban mobility.

Partnership Integrity & Disclosure

MoviSabio upholds the highest standards of institutional honesty: we clearly distinguish exploratory proposals from active pilots, and never publish unauthorized logos or unverified agreements.
