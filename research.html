Methodology & Rigor

Simulation Transparency & Verification Standards

In enterprise transportation engineering, mathematical claims must be rigorously qualified. All performance metrics reported by MoviSabio at this stage reflect in-silico microscopic simulation benchmarks inside calibrated digital twins:

Simulation Setup (SUMO & TraCI)

Networks are extracted from OpenStreetMap and calibrated with Krauss car-following models. In synthetic benchmark corridors, multi-agent DQN agents demonstrated up to 45% vehicular delay reductions compared to uncoordinated fixed-cycle timings.

Field Deployment Boundary

Physical field actuation requires municipal authorization, controller cabinet safety compliance (NTCIP 1202), and shadow-mode calibration. Simulated results serve to justify scoping trials, not claim closed municipal deployments.
