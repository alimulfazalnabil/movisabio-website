Virtual Fencing AgriTech

A specialized territorial solution concept applying geospatial polygon intelligence, GNSS boundaries, and IoT telemetry to replace physical rural barbed-wire fencing with dynamic, cloud-managed virtual pastures.

Spatial Polygon Architecture

Geospatial Boundaries Without Physical Fences

How MoviSabio's spatial engine translates satellite coordinate polygons into dynamic pasture management zones.

🗺️

1. Geospatial Monitoring & Polygon Boundaries

Farm operators draw arbitrary geometric zones directly on high-resolution satellite basemaps. Polygons define grazing paddocks, protected riparian forest buffers, and hazard exclusions without installing physical fence posts.

📡

2. Location-Based Animal Alerts

Designed to interface with GPS/LoRaWAN animal collar hardware. As animals approach boundary buffers, graduated auditory and benign sensory cues steer herds safely within designated grazing perimeters.

🚜

3. Agricultural Asset & Machinery Tracking

Extends beyond livestock to monitor heavy machinery, tractors, and remote irrigation equipment. Notifies farm managers when equipment leaves authorized zones or enters unharvested crop areas.

🌱

4. Rotational Grazing & Soil Health Restoration

Automates daily rotational grazing schedules, moving virtual fences automatically to prevent overgrazing, encourage deep root regeneration, and sequester carbon in alignment with ESG standards.
