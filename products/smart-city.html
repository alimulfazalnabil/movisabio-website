Core Capabilities • Deep Dive

Engineered for Municipal Resilience

Explore the deep technical features that make MoviSabio Smart City the preferred territorial intelligence platform for cities worldwide.

1. Micro-Simulation Digital Twin (SUMO / TraCI)

MoviSabio constructs 1:1 digital twin representations of real metropolitan corridors. Using Eclipse SUMO and Python TraCI, our platform models vehicle-following physics (Krauss car-following model), lane-changing behavior (LC2013), and multimodal transit interactions with second-by-second fidelity.

  • Simulates up to 100,000 concurrent agents on standard cloud compute.
  • Extracts sub-second latency telemetry for queue spillback prediction.
  • Tests scenario interventions (road closures, transit lanes) prior to real-world deployment.

2. AITCS Dynamic Signal Coordination

Our Adaptive Intelligent Traffic Control System (AITCS) combines deterministic arterial progression (MAXBAND) with adaptive reinforcement learning. Instead of fixed time-of-day tables, cycles dynamically expand and contract based on approaching vehicle platoons.

  • Simulated delay reductions of up to 31.4% along calibrated test corridors.
  • Coordinated green waves across multiple consecutive intersections.
  • Integrated Transit Signal Priority (TSP) for municipal bus fleets.

3. Edge Computer Vision (YOLOv8 — Phase 2)

Designed to turn existing municipal CCTV cameras into high-density traffic sensors without requiring expensive in-pavement inductive loops. Optimized for edge inference on NVIDIA Jetson and x86 edge servers.

  • Multi-class object tracking with DeepSORT / ByteTrack algorithms.
  • Zero raw video transmission; edge privacy guard strictly protects citizen anonymity.
  • Speed estimation and lane occupancy rate computed directly at the intersection.

4. Industrial Protocol Telemetry (MQTT & NTCIP 1202)

Connects the central platform to field traffic cabinets through industry-standard open protocols. The architecture supports NTCIP 1202 standards for signal controllers and utilizes lightweight MQTT over TLS 1.3 for resilient edge communication.

  • Sub-100ms telemetry heartbeat with automatic reconnection queues.
  • Deterministic fail-safe mode reverts controllers to local schedules if mesh disconnects.
  • Vendor-neutral design compatible with modern NEMA TS2 and 2070 hardware.
