Algorithmic Mechanics

How AITCS Optimizes Corridor Flow

Our dual-layer mathematical architecture merges deterministic traffic engineering rigor with modern artificial intelligence.

1

Lane-Level Traffic Analysis & Prediction

Continuously aggregates detector loop counts, optical tripwires, and queue occupancy rates. Time-series neural predictors project incoming vehicle arrival distributions 180 seconds into the future.

2

Deterministic Arterial Progression (MAXBAND)

Solves mixed-integer linear programming formulations to calculate optimal green-band progressions between adjacent intersections, ensuring vehicles moving at speed limit encounter zero red lights.

3

Reinforcement Learning Fine-Tuning (DQN)

Deep Q-Networks adaptively trim or extend phase splits by ±5 to 15 seconds to flush unscheduled side-street queues or grant immediate Transit Signal Priority (TSP) to approaching municipal buses.
