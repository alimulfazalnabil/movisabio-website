A forward-looking concept exploring how artificial intelligence can optimize municipal bus routes, predict dynamic passenger demand, enhance transit accessibility in underserved neighborhoods, and coordinate multimodal urban mobility networks.

Prospective Capabilities

Core Areas of Innovation

Exploring how spatial territorial intelligence can bridge the gap between static timetable schedules and real-time passenger realities.

🚏

1. Dynamic Route & Timetable Optimization

Investigates graph-based neural routing (A* and contraction hierarchies) to dynamically adjust bus headway frequencies during unexpected congestion, sporting events, or inclement weather, preventing bus bunching.

👥

2. Predictive Passenger Demand Modeling

Combines anonymized cellular mobility matrices with historical smart card / farebox data to model origin-destination (O-D) passenger flows, highlighting overlooked high-density travel corridors.

♿

3. Transit Equity & Neighborhood Accessibility

Measures the 15-minute isochrone accessibility of essential services (hospitals, schools, employment centers) across peripheral neighborhoods, providing municipal leaders with evidence-based investment maps.

🚲

4. Multimodal Mobility Planning

Integrates fixed-route bus and light-rail lines with micro-mobility (bike sharing, e-scooters) and pedestrian corridors, enabling seamless first-mile and last-mile connectivity.
