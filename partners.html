Engagement Methodology

6 Stages of Institutional Engagement

A structured, de-risked methodology designed to ensure transparent governance, rigorous technical validation, and zero disruption to active city operations.

Stage 01

Initial Institutional Discussion

Exploratory briefing with municipal leaders, transportation secretariats, or academic researchers to review institutional objectives, governance standards, and confidentiality terms.

Key Deliverable: Bilateral Scoping Charter & Mutual NDA / Terms of Reference.
Stage 02

Problem Identification & Corridor Scoping

Pinpointing specific arterial bottlenecks, high-congestion corridors, public transit delay zones, or elevated emission hotspots requiring targeted optimization.

Key Deliverable: Corridor Definition Document (5–10 intersections or key transit route).
Stage 03

Technical Feasibility Assessment

Reviewing existing controller cabinet hardware (NTCIP 1202, legacy controllers), communications network bandwidth, power availability, and CCTV camera placements.

Key Deliverable: Hardware Compatibility & Network Readiness Report.
Stage 04

Data & Infrastructure Assessment

Ingesting historical traffic counts, inductive loop detector data, OpenStreetMap road geometry, and signal timing sheets to calibrate base traffic patterns.

Key Deliverable: Calibrated Baseline Dataset & Data Sovereignty Protocol.
Stage 05

Pilot Proposal & Simulation Benchmarking

Constructing a high-fidelity SUMO digital twin corridor to simulate Deep RL adaptive signal timing against fixed-time and actuated baselines before field deployment.

Key Deliverable: Simulated KPI Benchmark (Projected delay & CO₂ reduction).
Stage 06

Evaluation & Collaboration Planning

Joint evaluation of simulation findings with municipal traffic engineers, execution of non-binding pilot Letter of Intent (LOI), and creation of the staged field deployment schedule.

Key Deliverable: Municipal Pilot LOI & Operational Phasing Roadmap.
