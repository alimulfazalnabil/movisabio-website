Partnerships & Open Innovation

Transforming territorial intelligence requires multi-institutional collaboration. We provide structured pathways for municipal transportation departments, university labs, technology integrators, and independent innovators.

🏛️

1. Municipal Letters of Intent (LOIs)

Designed for municipal departments of transportation, smart-city secretariats, and regional transit authorities seeking to test AI adaptive signal coordination on a designated 5–10 intersection arterial corridor.

Framework: Non-binding bilateral LOI • Pre-pilot SUMO digital twin simulation • Zero municipal hardware expenditure required.

2. Academic Research Collaborations

Partnering with university engineering faculties, intelligent transportation systems (ITS) centers, and geospatial research groups to co-author peer-reviewed benchmarks and grant proposals.

Framework: Open-source SUMO simulation data sharing • Joint grant submissions (Horizon Europe, National Science Foundations) • Graduate student internships.

⚙️

3. Technology & Controller Integrators

Collaborating with traffic signal cabinet manufacturers (NTCIP 1202 compliant), edge computing vendors, and CCTV camera suppliers to pre-certify MoviSabio edge inference software on municipal hardware.

Framework: NTCIP 1202 protocol verification • Edge SDK testing • Joint RFP bidding for regional municipal modernization contracts.

🌱

4. Climate Tech & ESG Consortia

Engaging with sustainability foundations, green transit alliances, and carbon accounting verifiers to validate real-time idle emission mitigation models against UN SDGs 11 and 13.

Framework: Emission reduction auditing • Low-Emission Zone (LEZ) reporting standardization • Non-dilutive climate grant alliances.
