TECHNOLOGY APPROACH

Connect data, models and operational context

A modular architecture is intended to combine geospatial foundations, data engineering, AI models and simulation environments without assuming that every integration is already deployed.

Conceptual architecture layers

  • Data sources: geospatial, mobility, sensor and contextual datasets
  • Data foundation: ingestion, validation, storage and spatial processing
  • Intelligence: analytics, forecasting and optimization experiments
  • Simulation: scenario testing and digital-twin workflows
  • Applications: dashboards, decision support and integration interfaces

Traffic coordination development note

The current AI traffic coordination work is simulation-first. A deterministic optimizer is active; LSTM is used for forecasting only; physical traffic-signal actuation is disabled. Results should be interpreted as simulation or development outputs, not field-proven performance.

Any future connection to live traffic infrastructure requires separate engineering validation, safety assessment, authorization and controlled pilot testing.
