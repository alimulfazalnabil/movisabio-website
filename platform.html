Core Subsystems & Operational Features

A granular review of each subsystem operating within MoviSabio Enterprise Smart City.

1. AITCS Adaptive Signal Timing

Continuously computes queue lengths and approach velocities to dynamically adjust green times. Rather than calculating single isolated intersections, our algorithm optimizes green waves across entire 10-to-20 intersection corridors.

Fail-Safe Constraint: Minimum green and maximum red times are hard-locked into the controller firmware, preventing edge AI model drift from compromising pedestrian safety.

2. Edge Vision & Vehicle Classification

Ingests standard H.264/H.265 video from traffic cameras. Real-time inference classifies objects into cars, delivery trucks, city buses, motorcycles, bicycles, and pedestrians, converting visual pixels into structured telemetry.

Privacy by Design: Video frames are processed locally at the edge. No faces or private vehicle data are stored on central cloud databases, ensuring GDPR and LGPD compliance.

3. Digital Twin Simulation (SUMO & CARLA)

Every city network is ingested from OpenStreetMap into the SUMO (Simulation of Urban MObility) framework. Operators can simulate major detours, road closures, or sporting events in a virtual sandbox with synthetic vehicle demand.

Benchmark Validation: Enables municipal departments to evaluate expected delay reductions in software before authorizing physical controller deployment.

4. Automated Emergency Preemption

Synchronizes with municipal CAD (Computer-Aided Dispatch) feeds. When an emergency vehicle is en route, the platform automatically clears downstream vehicle queues and secures green phases through the intersection.

Operational Impact: Targets up to 30% reduction in emergency travel times, reducing intersection crash risks for first responders.
Implementation Methodology

4-Phase Municipal Rollout Framework

A structured, low-risk deployment framework designed to prove measurable results before scaling city-wide.

PHASE 01 • WEEKS 1–4
Digital Twin Modeling

Ingest city network from OpenStreetMap. Calibrate baseline traffic flows using municipal sensor logs inside the SUMO simulation engine.

PHASE 02 • WEEKS 5–8
Corridor Sandbox (Shadow Mode)

Connect edge hardware to 5–10 intersections. The AI observes real-time CCTV feeds and computes optimal timings in "shadow mode" without physical actuation.

PHASE 03 • WEEKS 9–14
Active Pilot Actuation

Activate dynamic signal adjustment during off-peak hours first, expanding to 24/7 autonomous coordination under strict MAXBAND fail-safe constraints.

PHASE 04 • WEEKS 15+
City-Wide Expansion

Unveil the 3D Digital Twin Command Center at City Hall. Expand AI coordination across adjacent arterial corridors and connect public transit priority.
