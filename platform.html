Section B • Core Engine

Traffic Intelligence & Signal Coordination

Combining classical traffic engineering mathematics with modern artificial intelligence to deliver adaptive, multi-intersection signal timing that guarantees vehicular safety and maximizes arterial progression.

Multi-intersection corridor coordination: Dynamic green-wave synchronization adjusting phase splits based on real-time vehicle approach velocities.

1. AI Traffic Control System (AITCS)

IMPLEMENTED / VERIFIED

Dynamically allocates green time to traffic movements exhibiting the highest cumulative delay and queue density. Operates continuously without requiring manual re-timing schedules.

Inputs:Lane vehicle counts, approach speeds, queue dwell times
Logic:Actuated phase extension with minimum/maximum green bounds
Outputs:NTCIP 1202 phase split commands, hold/force-off triggers
Status:Verified in SUMO microscopic simulation loop

2. Hierarchical Hybrid Framework (MAXBAND + AI)

IMPLEMENTED / VERIFIED

Our core algorithmic innovation: classical MAXBAND linear programming calculates deterministic arterial green waves, while Deep Reinforcement Learning agents adjust local splits within certified safety bounds.

MAXBAND Objective: Maximize (b + k * b_bar)
subject to: C_min ≤ C ≤ C_max, red_clearance ≥ min_yellow + all_red
Safety:Precludes AI model hallucination; hard safety envelope enforced
Dependencies:Python PuLP / SciPy Optimization, TraCI API

3. Multi-Intersection Corridor Coordination

ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

Coordinates a chain of 5 to 20 consecutive signalized intersections to create bi-directional green waves along primary arterial routes, mitigating shockwaves caused by mid-block queue spillovers.

Inputs:Upstream departure rates, travel times, downstream queue storage
Logic:Offset optimization based on progression speed and platoon dispersion
Status:Active training on multi-agent SUMO grid models

4. Emergency Vehicle Preemption (EVP)

PLANNED

Interfaces with emergency dispatch Computer-Aided Dispatch (CAD) systems. Preemptively clears downstream vehicular queues and provides a continuous green wave for ambulances and fire engines.

Target:Up to 30% reduction in emergency travel times
Protocols:NTCIP 1202 EVP standard with automatic phase recovery
Section C • Visual Sensor Network

Computer Vision & Edge AI Processing

Transforming existing municipal CCTV and traffic camera feeds into structured, real-time vehicular and pedestrian telemetry without purchasing new physical road sensors.

YOLOv8 Edge Computer Vision: Multi-class vehicle detection, lane-wise spatial counting, and pedestrian crosswalk occupancy tracking.

YOLOv8 Real-Time Vehicle Detection

IMPLEMENTED / VERIFIED

Processes H.264 / H.265 RTSP streams from city cameras. Detects and tracks vehicles with high precision across diverse lighting and weather conditions (sun glare, rain, nighttime).

Classes:Cars, delivery vans, city buses, heavy trucks, motorcycles, bicycles
Frameworks:PyTorch, Ultralytics YOLOv8, OpenCV 4.9
Latency:< 35ms per frame on edge hardware (NVIDIA Jetson Orin)

Lane-Wise Spatial Counting & Speed Tracking

IMPLEMENTED / VERIFIED

Defines virtual polygon detection zones and directional tripwires across approach lanes to compute vehicle density, approach speed, queue dwell time, and Level of Service (LOS).

Algorithm:ByteTrack / DeepSORT tracking with perspective transformation
Outputs:Lane occupancy percentage, vehicle speed distribution, queue lengths

Edge Deployment & Zero Hardware Upgrade

ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

Inference runs locally on ruggedized edge micro-servers installed in signal cabinets, eliminating the cost of streaming gigabytes of raw video to the cloud and ensuring resilience during network outages.

Hardware:Compatible with NVIDIA Jetson Orin / Industrial ARM64 nodes
Telemetry:Transmits lightweight JSON metrics via MQTT over secure VPN

Automatic Number Plate Recognition (ANPR) Roadmap

PLANNED (PRIVACY SAFEGUARDED)

Planned capability for point-to-point travel time estimation and municipal security. Strictly engineered with cryptographic hashing and local redaction to ensure GDPR and LGPD compliance.

Safeguards:Zero raw plate storage; one-way salted cryptographic hashing
Governance:Requires explicit municipal authorization and legal compliance review

Section G • Deep Learning Architecture

AI Prediction & Reinforcement Learning

Harnessing sequential deep learning for multi-horizon traffic forecasting and multi-agent reinforcement learning for continuous signal timing optimization.

AI Analytics Dashboard: Multi-horizon LSTM traffic demand predictions, corridor travel time distributions, and reinforcement learning policy convergence metrics.

LSTM / GRU Multi-Horizon Forecasting

ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

Recurrent neural networks ingest historical and rolling traffic time-series to forecast corridor congestion levels 10, 30, and 60 minutes in advance, enabling preemptive signal offset adjustments.

Architecture:2-Layer Bidirectional LSTM with Attention Mechanism
Horizons:T+10 min (tactical), T+30 min (corridor), T+60 min (strategic)
Framework:PyTorch, TorchScript optimized for production serving

Multi-Agent Deep Reinforcement Learning (DQN / PPO)

ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT

Each intersection operates an autonomous reinforcement learning agent. The state space captures approach queue lengths and waiting times; the reward function penalizes aggregate intersection delay and phase-switching overhead.

Reward: R_t = - ∑ (w_1 * queue_i + w_2 * delay_i) - w_3 * phase_switch
Algorithms:Deep Q-Network (DQN) with Prioritized Experience Replay; PPO
Coordination:Neighborhood state sharing across adjacent arterial controllers
